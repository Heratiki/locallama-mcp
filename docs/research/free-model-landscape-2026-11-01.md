> **Status: second run.** Every row below was written from a page fetched live on 2026-11-01 (see **Method** at the end). The first (DRAFT) numbers are gone. If a page did not give a value, the cell says `not published` (the provider shows no number on the page I fetched) or `unconfirmed` (I could not find it on a primary page). > Context: LocalLama MCP helps people with no money code with free online models and small local models on an old laptop (8 GB RAM, no GPU). Dates in "source" cells are the day I read the page (all 2026-11-01, local time -04:00).

**How to read the "terms allow automated/agent use?" column:** I only write `yes` when a page says so. Most providers sell an API, so scripted calls are the normal use, but I did not find a sentence that says "agents are allowed" for most of them. Where I found only the product's nature, the cell says so.

**Facts that change older plans (found on the live pages):**
- **GitHub Models is retired** (docs say fully retired as of July 30, 2026). It cannot be a provider.
- **Gemini free-tier request and token limits are not on the docs page.** The rate-limits page says to look at AI Studio. Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 3.1 Pro are not on the free tier.
- **Gemini CLI no longer has a free tier.** Free individual access moved to Antigravity CLI on June 18, 2026.
- **Cerebras has no permanent free tier** (a $5 / 30-day trial, and it needs a payment method).
- **Together has no free trial** (minimum $15 credit purchase).

## Takeaways for LocalLama

**G7: first free providers to add** (all expose an API key and a chat endpoint; none needs more than an account unless noted)
1. **OpenRouter `:free`**: published limits (20 RPM; 50 RPD, or 1,000 RPD after 10 credits), OpenAI-compatible, several free models with 256K to 1M context including `cohere/north-mini-code:free`.
2. **Groq Free plan**: published limits (30 RPM, 1,000 RPD, 8K TPM, 200K TPD) on `gpt-oss-120b`, `gpt-oss-20b` and `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` with 131,072 context; OpenAI-compatible. The 8K TPM cap is small for agent loops.
3. **Google Gemini API free tier**: `gemini-3.8-flash` is free of charge with a 1M-token window and an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Limits are not published outside AI Studio, and the free tier lets Google use and human-review your prompts, so LocalLama must show that warning.
4. **Cloudflare Workers AI free allowance**: 10,000 Neurons/day, 300 RPM for text generation, `gpt-oss-20b` with 128,000 context, OpenAI-compatible. Paid-only models (kimi, glm, deepseek) must be filtered out.
5. **SambaNova Free Tier** (reserve): no payment method needed, but only 20 RPM, 20 RPD and 200K tokens/day; good as a fallback for `gpt-oss-120b` and `DeepSeek-V3.1`.

Left out and why: GitHub Models (retired), Cerebras (needs a payment method; trial ends in 30 days), Together (min $15), Hugging Face (free users have no credits), Mistral and NVIDIA (limits not published; re-check before adding), Cohere (1,000 calls/month).

**G8: local models for the 8 GB, CPU-only profile**
1. **Granite 4.2 3B, Q4_K_M (2.24 GB)**: Apache-2.0, 128K context, LiveCodeBench v6 69.71, official GGUF; the best-sourced 3B choice.
2. **LFM3-1.7B, Q4_K_M (1.10 GB)**: smallest file, LiveCodeBench v6 73.46 and SWE-bench Verified 43.40, 256K context; needs a recent llama.cpp build and its custom licence must be read first.
